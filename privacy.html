We do not share your data with anyone except the service providers we rely on to run the business, who act on our instructions:

  • Our accountants receive invoicing records.

We may also disclose data where the law requires it.

International transfers

Google and GitHub may store data outside the UK, including in the United States. Where they do, the transfer is covered by the UK–US data bridge or by the UK’s approved contractual safeguards built into their terms.

How we protect it

Access is limited to Ontonomic’s partners. All accounts are protected by two-step verification, and we use the security controls provided by Google Workspace.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the data we hold about you
  • correct anything inaccurate
  • delete your data, where we have no reason to keep it
  • restrict or object to how we use it
  • send your data to you or another organisation in a portable format

Email enquiries@ontonomic.com to make a request. We will reply within one month. There is no charge.

Cookies

This website does not set cookies. If that changes, we will update this notice and ask for your consent before setting any non-essential cookies.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.
